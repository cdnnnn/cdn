Suggestion:

Could the organization identify employees with unique skills, expertise, or interests and provide opportunities for them to contribute to strategic initiatives beyond their current roles? For example, employees with strong expertise in a particular technology, business area, or emerging field could be invited to contribute to relevant organizational initiatives or cross-functional projects.

Why it matters:

This could help the organization make better use of the skills and knowledge already available within the company. It can also bring diverse perspectives to strategic initiatives, encourage employees to take greater ownership, and create opportunities for people to contribute beyond their day-to-day responsibilities. Over time, this could help build stronger internal capabilities and support the organization's future growth.
